Company Overview

Building Safer Environments

Founded in 2019, Jofinitycore Systems Ltd has grown from a small technical team into one of Kenya's most respected security solutions providers. We operate from our head office in Ruiru, with field teams covering the entire country.

Our portfolio spans residential estates, commercial complexes, manufacturing plants, educational institutions and healthcare facilities. Whatever the scale, we bring the same commitment to quality and professionalism.
