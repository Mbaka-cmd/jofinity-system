CCTV Installation & Systems

Our CCTV solutions cover everything from single-camera home setups to multi-site enterprise surveillance networks. We supply and install HD, 4K, PTZ and specialised cameras including thermal imaging and low-light models.

All systems include remote viewing via mobile app, cloud or NVR storage, and integrate with access control and alarm platforms for a unified security ecosystem.

  • HD, 4K and IP camera systems
  • Night vision and low-light technology
  • Remote monitoring via mobile app
  • Cloud and on-site NVR/DVR storage
  • PTZ cameras with auto-tracking
  • Analytics: motion detection, people counting, ANPR

Perimeter Security

Electric Fencing Systems

Energised perimeter fencing provides one of the most effective deterrents against intruders. Our systems deliver a safe but memorable shock, trigger alarms instantly and can be monitored remotely via GSM.

We supply and install Nemtek and Gallagher energisers with tamper-detection, zone mapping and smartphone alerts, covering residential, commercial and industrial perimeters.

  • High-voltage energiser systems
  • GSM remote monitoring and alerts
  • Zone-based mapping for large perimeters
  • Tamper and short-circuit detection
  • Compatibility with CCTV and alarm systems
  • Compliance with local safety standards
Nemtek  ·  Gallagher

Intrusion Detection

Alarm Systems

Our intrusion detection systems use a network of motion sensors, door/window contacts, glass break detectors and panic buttons to provide instant alerts on any security breach, day or night.

Systems can be linked to a professional monitoring station for rapid response, or configured to send SMS/push notifications directly to your phone or security personnel.

  • PIR and dual-tech motion detectors
  • Wireless and wired configurations
  • Panic buttons for immediate response
  • Professional monitoring centre integration
  • SMS, email and push notifications
  • Pet-immune sensors available
DSC  ·  Bosch  ·  Paradox

Biometric Systems

Biometric technology provides the highest level of identity verification, ensuring only authorised individuals access sensitive areas. We install fingerprint, facial recognition and iris scanner systems for access control and time-attendance management.

All systems integrate with HR and payroll software to automate attendance tracking and eliminate buddy-punching.

  • Fingerprint recognition terminals
  • Facial recognition access control
  • Iris scanners for high-security areas
  • Time and attendance management
  • Payroll software integration
  • Cloud and on-premise management
